na·vi1

/ˈnɑː·vi/  ·  noun · from the Hebrew נָבִיא

1

Voice

One who speaks, communicates or carries a message.

House of Navi is, at heart, a storytelling organisation. The ambition isn't to give people a voice: the communities we work with already have one. It is to create the platform, storytelling and reach that let those voices, ideas and initiatives travel further.
